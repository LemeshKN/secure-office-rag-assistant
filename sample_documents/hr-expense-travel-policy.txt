Expense and Travel Policy

Owner: Finance and HR. Applies to all employees.

Claims: Submit an expense claim in the expense tool within 30 days of the expense. Attach receipts. A claim over 25 dollars without a receipt is rejected.

Approval: A manager approves claims up to 500 dollars. A director approves claims above 500 dollars. Finance pays within 10 working days after approval.

Flights: Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights over 6 hours with manager approval. Book at least 14 days ahead when possible.

Hotels: The limit is 180 dollars per night in major cities and 120 dollars per night elsewhere.

Meals: The allowance is 50 dollars per day while traveling. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

Learning budget: Each employee has 800 dollars per year for courses and conferences. The manager must approve each request.

Home office: Remote employees get a one-time 300 dollars for equipment.
