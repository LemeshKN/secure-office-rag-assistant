Hiring and Interview Process

Owner: Talent Acquisition. For hiring managers and interviewers.

Approval: A new position needs an approved headcount request from the director and Finance before the job post goes out.

Interview steps:
1. Resume screen by the recruiter.
2. Phone screen, 30 minutes.
3. Technical interview, 60 minutes.
4. Team interview, 45 minutes.
5. Hiring manager interview, 45 minutes.

Panel: Each panel includes at least one interviewer from outside the hiring team.

Scorecards: Every interviewer fills in the scorecard and submits feedback within 24 hours. Interviewers do not discuss a candidate before they submit feedback.

Decision: A hire needs at least 3 yes votes and no strong no vote.

Offers: The hiring manager prepares the offer. HR approves the pay within the salary band. An offer stays valid for 5 working days.

Time to hire: The target is 45 days from job post to signed offer.

Background check: The candidate must clear a background check before the start date.

Referral bonus: An employee gets a referral bonus of 1,000 dollars after the referred hire completes 90 days.

Questions to avoid: Never ask about age, marital status, religion or family plans.
