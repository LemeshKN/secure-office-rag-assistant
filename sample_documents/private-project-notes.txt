Private Project Notes: Apollo

Project Apollo is the internal tool that lets support staff search customer tickets. The project lead is Priya. The team has three engineers.

Milestones: The first prototype is due on 15 December. The beta release is planned for 20 January. The final launch is planned for 28 February.

Budget: The Apollo project has a cloud budget of 4,000 dollars for this quarter. About 1,200 dollars has been spent so far on servers and testing.

Decisions: The team chose PostgreSQL for storage. The team decided to hold a review meeting every Thursday at 10 AM.
