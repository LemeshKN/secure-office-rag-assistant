AI Model Evaluation Standards

Owner: AI Lead. Applies to every change to a model, prompt or retrieval setting.

Gate: No model or prompt change reaches production until it passes the offline evaluation set.

Evaluation set: The golden set has 200 questions, each with a reference document. It lives in the repo folder eval/golden. Changes to the golden set need review by two people. Nobody edits it to make a result pass.

Retrieval: The correct document must appear in the top 5 results for at least 85 percent of questions.

Groundedness: The average groundedness score must be at least 0.80. No more than 5 percent of answers may contain an unsupported claim.

Refusals: The set includes 30 questions that no document answers. The system must refuse at least 90 percent of them.

Latency: The p95 latency for chat answers must stay under 6 seconds.

Cost: The average cost per answer must not rise by more than 20 percent compared with the last release.

Model changes: A model change needs a written comparison of the old and new model on the same set. The AI Lead reviews it.

Tracking: Log each result in the experiment tracker with the commit hash.

Human review: The AI team reviews a sample of 25 production answers every month.
