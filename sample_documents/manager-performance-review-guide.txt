Manager Guide: Performance Reviews

Owner: HR Business Partner. For people managers.

Cycle: Reviews run twice a year, in June and in December.

Timeline: Employees submit a self-assessment 10 working days before their review meeting. Managers send a draft rating 5 working days before the meeting. Managers collect feedback from at least 3 peers.

Goals: Each employee sets 3 to 5 goals at the start of the cycle. Managers check progress against these goals.

Rating scale: 1 means needs improvement. 3 means meets expectations. 5 means outstanding. No more than 10 percent of a team may receive a 5.

Calibration: Directors and HR compare ratings across teams in a calibration meeting before ratings are final.

Promotions: A promotion needs a written case with at least 2 examples of work at the next level. The director and HR must approve it.

Performance improvement plan: A plan lasts 60 days with a check-in every 2 weeks. The manager must inform HR before a plan starts.

One-to-ones: Managers hold a 30 minute one-to-one with each direct report every week.

Confidentiality: Ratings and salary decisions are confidential. Managers deliver them in person or in a call, not by email.
